## bloub

Mimo's animated avatar (`ios/Ryoko/Avatar/Engine/`) is a Swift port of the avatar
engine in bloub (`src/bot/`), https://github.com/jeremy-prt/bloub, ported from commit
`b4bb3c1b5f93c7b87a2e8d620f667c4093d97749`. bloub is an SVG recreation of the x.ai bot
avatar. Ryoko and bloub are not affiliated with, endorsed by or connected to x.ai.
Ryoko uses its own body shape, rest expression and monochrome rendering
(`ios/Ryoko/Avatar/MimoAvatarStyle.swift`).
